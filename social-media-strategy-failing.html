The Hard Truth

99% of content falls flat.
You're not the problem — your approach is.

5 Billion Creators
In 2005: a few million people. Today: nearly 5 billion people on social media creating content. The noise is unprecedented.
It's Not Shadowbanning
Algorithms aren't your enemy. They're a business machine looking for content that grabs and holds attention at scale.
The Old Paradigm
Niche content for niche audiences no longer works. Algorithms won't do heavy lifting for content only a small segment cares about.
The Insight
It's not the content — it's the context. How you represent what you know determines whether anyone ever sees it.
Algorithm Truth

The algorithm wants to partner with you.

"These algorithms have a fundamental goal: find content that grabs and holds attention at scale — to keep people on their platform longer. They want to be our best friend."

— Brendan Kane
How Platforms Make Money
Serving ads → they need eyes on screen → they need your great content to keep people watching.
Unlike Netflix
Netflix spends billions on originals. Platforms rely on creators as the fuel. Win-win — if your content earns attention.
The 3-Second Rule

Stop the scroll. Then hold it.

3s
Instagram / TikTok
A view is counted at 3 seconds. This is when intent begins — anything before is noise.
30s
YouTube
Click thumbnail → watch 30 seconds = one view. Higher bar, but far deeper engagement signal.
∞
Retention
Grabbing attention is just the start. Holding it — through full retention — is what drives distribution.

No clickbait. No tricks. Just content that genuinely earns every second of watch time.

Core Strategy

The Generalist Principle

"Make your content interesting to the average person — without losing the subtext of your core audience."

— Brendan Kane
  • Taxes, real estate, nutrition, finance — any topic can go viral when contextualized for general curiosity.
  • You don't need to change who you are — just how you frame it for a broader audience.
  • A larger audience means even a small % of converts outperforms 100% of a niche audience.
Communication Model

Social media is one-to-one,
not one-to-many.

❌ Wrong Mental Model
Standing on a stage, broadcasting to millions. This mindset kills personal connection — and performance.
✓ Right Mental Model
Speaking directly to one person watching alone on their phone. That intimacy is what drives engagement and virality.

"Most people communicate on social media as if they're standing on a stage — but that really drives performance down. You're not creating that personal connection."

— Brendan Kane
The Hook System

Going viral starts before you hit record.

01
Research 50 Hook Ideas
Scan Google Trends, viral headlines, top-performing clips in your space. Don't stop at the first idea that comes to mind.
02
Score Each Hook 1–5
Have your team rate viral potential. Surface the top 5–10 ideas with the highest scores before committing to a single one.
03
AB-Test Thumbnails & Headlines
Use the YouTube built-in tester or Facebook ad platform. Test up to 100 variations. Measure click-through rate — not gut feel.
04
Focus on the Output Format
Ask: what does this look like as a short-form clip? Joe Rogan and Lex Fridman's massive shows are driven by their clips, not the full interview.
Virality → Commerce

Turn attention into scalable revenue.

Personal Brand > Company Brand
Company accounts default to "creating ads." Personal accounts unlock authentic storytelling — and far stronger conversion.
Consistent Format = Predictable Growth
Find a format that has gone viral 5+ times for the same creator — then model and execute it at scale.
Real Results (No Follower Head Start)
A hand surgeon with zero social experience → 700K TikTok followers, book deal, TV deal. Format choice made all the difference.
Matt Rife Model
Mastered the output format for crowd-work clips. Result: $25M/year. Virality as a distribution engine, not a vanity metric.
Action Plan

5 steps for $2M–$5M businesses
ready to break through.

1 · Mindset
Commit to social media as a real growth lever. $500/month to a manager won't produce $10M results.
2 · Find Your Format
Study outside your industry. An insurance agent can learn from a nutritionist's format.
3 · Build Trust, Not Ads
Organic content = know, like, trust. Ads are separate. Don't blur the two channels.
4 · Test Repeatability
Only adopt a format that the same creator has used to go viral 5+ times.
5 · Analyze Every Single Video
Compare your high-performers vs low-performers side by side. Watch them. List every difference. Apply the lessons to the next video. Repeat.

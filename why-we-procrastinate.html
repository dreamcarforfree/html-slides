The Myth of Laziness

Procrastination is Emotional

  • It's not about being lazy or unmotivated
  • It's an instinctive escape from task discomfort
  • Vague, difficult, or boring tasks trigger brain friction
  • Phones are simply convenient escape hatches from feelings
The Strategy

How to Stop Avoiding

Reframe & Reduce
Inject curiosity & novelty into the task. Break goals into micro-steps.

Surf the Urge
Ride out discomfort spikes for 10 minutes before distracting.

Make Distractions Hard
Physically lock away devices or block time-sucking apps.

Leverage Social Connection
Align your commitments with accountability partners.

Hack 1: Friction

Reframe & Shrink the Task

  • Curiosity cures boredom — look at the task as a puzzle
  • Break goals aggressively into "Starter Phases"
  • Focus on writing one sentence, not the whole page
  • Once you start, momentum naturally takes over
Fogg's Behavior Model

Crossing the Action Line

Task Success Zone

Action occurs when: Motivation x Ability x Prompt aligns.

Increase ability by shrinking the task. Shift difficult work into the Easy zone!

Block the Escape Hatch

Smartphones occupy the success zone because they are *too* easy.

Force distractions out by increasing their friction (lock apps, hide phones).

Hack 2: Urge Surfing

Dopamine & The 10-Min Rule

  • Dopamine spikes *before* we cave to distraction
  • Ride the discomfort wave using the 10-Minute Rule
  • If you resist the spike, the craving naturally subsides
  • Reward your restraint with goal-aligned treats
Hack 3: Social Connection

Leverage Your Social Nature

  • Humans are highly motivated to fulfill social promises
  • Accountability partners dramatically increase follow-through
  • Shift focus from "I must do this" to "I will inspire others"
  • Designing behavior works better than brute willpower
Execution

Your Quick Action Plan

  • 1. Identify the task & pinpoint the exact discomfort
  • 2. Reframe with a playful twist or curiosity query
  • 3. Shrink to the smallest step: e.g. open the doc
  • 4. Surf the urge: wait 10 mins before checking phones

No More Avoiding

"Boredom is best cured by curiosity."

Design your behavior, embrace curiosity, and take that first microscopic step today.
